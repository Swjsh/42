## LIVE DEFECT — a broker read hang silently delays the exit loop (found 13:04-13:20 ET)

### What happened

bold-2 entered 5x SPY260813P00776000 @ 0.64 at 12:41 ET (a PUT -- the engine flipped bearish
after the morning calls). Its -50%% catastrophe stop sat at 0.32.

Between roughly 12:55 and 13:12 ET, bold-2's `/v2/positions` and `/v2/account` endpoints HUNG:

    /v2/clock      OK  0.2s      <- network + auth fine
    /v2/orders     OK  0.2s      <- fine
    /v2/positions  TimeoutError 15.1s
    /v2/account    TimeoutError 15.1s

Only bold-2. safe-2 and risky-1 answered all three in 0.1-0.2s throughout.

During that window:
  - `bold-2/exit-state.json` went 14 minutes without refreshing while the engine ticked every 60s
  - a full tick was observed to pass with NO refresh
  - NO stop sell was attempted, while the bid (0.28) was already through the 0.32 stop
  - every tick still logged `exit=0`, and nothing appeared in any error log

### The mechanism

A position read that times out is swallowed, so the arm is skipped and the tick reports success.
**exit=0 does not mean "the arm was managed"; it means "nothing raised".** Same class as the
window-leak detector that reported `leaks_total: 0` across 2,997,000 polls this morning while
console windows were visibly flashing -- twice in one day, a green signal over a live failure.

Contributing: a persistence probe measured 4/6 successes with one taking **24.0s**, while
`fleet_broker` uses `timeout=15`. So even a RECOVERING endpoint fails that read.

### Cost, measured

The engine recovered and fired the stop at 13:12 ET once the endpoint came back.
**The position was DELAYED, not abandoned** -- an earlier in-session characterisation of it as
"unmanaged" was wrong and is corrected here.

    entry 12:41 @ 0.64   exit 13:12 @ 0.24   qty 5
    realized  -$200.00  (-62.5%%)
    stop level 0.32 -> filled 12.5 percentage points BEYOND it
    slippage attributable to the delay:  -$40.00

Bounded today only because the position was small and the move was slow. The same mechanism on
a faster move, or through 15:50, is not bounded -- and if the hang had persisted through
EodFlatten the 0DTE put would have expired for the full -$320.

### Fix (NOT applied -- Rule 9, live session)

1. A failed position read must NOT be silently equivalent to "nothing to do". It needs a retry
   with backoff, and on exhaustion a loud flag -- not `exit=0`.
2. `fleet_broker`'s 15s timeout is below the observed 24s recovery latency. Raising it alone is
   a TUNING fix and does not address (1); do both or neither.
3. Per-arm exit-state staleness belongs on the health surface: an arm holding a position whose
   exit-state has not refreshed in > N ticks is the alarm that would have caught this in 60s
   instead of 14 minutes.
