## DATA GAPS

1. **context_bundle feature-availability boundary.** `trend_alignment`/`alignment_score`
   shipped 2026-07-14; `levels_context`/`today_context`/`prior_day` shipped 2026-07-15
   (per the live `context_bundle.note` field). The single 2026-07-13 episode (FLEET-LOOSE-R,
   -$25) therefore has `context_bundle: null` — no alignment_score or level-distance value
   is fabricated for it; it's bucketed "n/a" in both cross-tabs above. This is a real
   feature-rollout boundary, not a join failure.

2. **2 manual (J-called) episodes have no engine decision row.** 2026-07-15 13:16:56 ET
   (-$60, CORE-SAFE) and 2026-07-17 12:04:29 ET (+$89, CORE-SAFE) were placed outside the
   scored heartbeat path (no `exec`/`extra_exec` entry in `core-decisions.jsonl` for their
   order id — consistent with the `j_intent_executor` manual path). Both are joined via
   `nearest_minute_manual_trade_no_engine_decision_row` to the closest same-account decision
   tick (deltas of 0.1min and 0.4min) — this captures *what the engine's scorer saw at that
   moment* ("no setup passed scoring" in both cases), not a decision that led to the trade,
   since none exists. Setup is honestly reported as UNKNOWN, not backfilled with a guess.

3. **Fleet-arm market context is a cross-account proxy, not a same-account read.**
   Fleet decisions.jsonl (safe-3/risky-1/risky-3) carries setup/quality/trigger_level but
   no ribbon/VIX/bear_score/bull_score/context_bundle — those fields don't exist in the
   fleet schema (fleet arms consume the shared underlying signal, not their own scored
   tick). All 10 fleet episodes are joined to the nearest-time CORE (safe or bold) decision
   row instead (all within delta ≤ 1 min in this window — firm_brief.py's own docstring
   confirms fleet arms fire "the SAME signal across accounts within the same second").
   Flagged via `join_quality` suffix `+core_context_proxy` and `context.context_source` in
   the JSON so this is never silently conflated with an exact same-account read.

4. **No episodes lost to unresolvable joins.** Every one of the 27 episodes has *some*
   context (0 `no_join` results); `n_no_order_id=0` across the whole run. 15 core episodes
   are `exact_order_match` (their own account's scored decision row), 10 fleet episodes are
   `fleet_own_setup_exact+core_context_proxy`, 2 manual episodes are nearest-minute fallback.

5. **trades.csv cross-check.** The pre-existing `journal/trades.csv` rows written by
   `fleet_journal_bridge.py` for this window (from the same `pnl-statement.json` source)
   independently show the same 40 leg-level rows for the arms/dates in scope — spot-checked
   against the 2026-07-17 and 2026-07-20 tail rows before building this dataset; no
   discrepancy found.

## Reconciliation

Per-day, per-arm P&L summed from the 27 episodes reproduces `pnl-statement.json`'s own
`per_day` field to within $0.02 (the residual is BTC-dust rounding on days with a
crypto-twin settlement leg, already excluded from the option dataset — see DATA GAPS #
n/a, confirmed by hand below). Two anchors were supplied to check against:

### 2026-07-17: core ≈ +$342 live / +$679 all-arms — MATCHES exactly

- Core engine-only (excludes the 1 manual +$89 trade): safe-2 engine +$151 + bold-2 engine
  +$191 = **+$342**. Matches.
- All arms, all attribution (core engine + core manual + all 3 fleet arms): safe-2 +$240
  (engine +151, manual +89) + bold-2 +$191 + safe-3 $0 + risky-1 $0 + risky-3 +$248 =
  **+$679**. Matches.

### 2026-07-20: safe = -$111 (anchor) vs -$141 (this dataset, and `pnl-statement.json`,
and the day's own `journal/2026-07-20.md` firm-brief line) — DISCREPANCY, investigated

`pnl-statement.json` (generated 2026-07-20T20:05:02Z / 16:05 ET, i.e. after the 15:55 ET
close) and `journal/2026-07-20.md`'s own firm-brief section ("Total recorded P&L: -141")
both independently say **-$141**, not -$111. Root cause of the gap, found by summing the
episodes in entry-time order:

| # | Entry-exit (ET) | Setup | P&L | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 09:51:24-09:52:03 | vix_regime_dayside | -45 | -45 |
| 2 | 09:54:19-09:55:04 | vix_regime_dayside | -18 | -63 |
| 3 | 09:55:24-09:56:03 | vix_regime_dayside | -24 | -87 |
| 4 | 14:01:31-14:16:03 | BEARISH_REJECTION_RIDE_THE_RIBBON | -24 | **-111** |
| 5 | 14:49:20-14:53:03 | bollinger_squeeze | -30 | **-141** |

The running total through episode #4's 14:16 ET exit is exactly **-$111** — matching the
anchor to the dollar. Episode #5 (bollinger_squeeze, entered 14:49 ET, exited 14:53 ET,
-$30) closes AFTER that point. **Conclusion: the -$111 anchor is an intraday snapshot
taken between 14:16 ET and 14:49 ET, before the day's final trade closed; -$141 is the
true EOD total once that last fill is included.** No data-quality issue in this dataset —
the two numbers describe two different points in the day, and -$141 is the complete one
(verified against 2 independent sources: `pnl-statement.json` and the day's own
firm-brief). Flagging this explicitly since the task called for investigation before
writing, not silent acceptance of either number.
