# CONVICTION SCORE — historical backtest on real broker fills (2026-08-12)

**Verdict: the score is NULL on history, and the floor-5 ratchet is NOT DEMONSTRATED to help.
It is not a kill either — the entire high-conviction evidence base is 6 independent signals.**

- Rank correlation between conviction and size-neutral return, at the honest unit of
  observation (one row per *signal*, not per mirrored fill): **-0.03** (level-anchored) /
  **-0.09** (all). That is zero. The per-*fill* figure of -0.17 is inflated ~2.3x by arm
  fan-out and should not be quoted.
- The floor-5 forward ratchet suppresses **-$22** of P&L. A matched random-suppression null
  removing the same number of trades from the same (arm, day) cells has median **+$159**,
  p05 **-$487**. **p = 0.33.** The rule's "savings" is the base rate.
- Every floor from 2 to 6 leaves the KEPT cohort **worse** than the unfiltered population on
  expectancy, win rate, and return-on-premium. There is no floor at which this score helps.
- **F3 PASSES** (88.3% of entries blocked at a flat floor 5; 89.8% under the forward ratchet)
  — inside the >95% DOA boundary, but only just, and it keeps 21 of 205 fills / 6 of 88 signals.

The prior verdict of **"NOT BACKTESTABLE"** in
[CONVICTION-RATCHET-DESIGN-2026-08-12.md](../deep-research/2026-08-12-churn/CONVICTION-RATCHET-DESIGN-2026-08-12.md)
**is corrected**: the level records were never persisted, but every producer of them is a pure
function of bar history with an injectable-bars seam, so they regenerate as-of. The
regeneration is measured against 28 real point-in-time `key-levels.json` snapshots, not assumed.

## 1. How history was made answerable

| need | source | how |
|---|---|---|
| level RECORDS (label / memory_score / touches / role) | regenerated as-of | `backtest/lib/reconstruct_levels_asof.py` (intraday + shelf, already RED-proofed causal) + `level_memory_producer.build_levels(df)` + `refresh_levels_intraday._merge_memory_levels` + PRIOR_DAY H/L/C |
| entry's trigger level (C1) | ledger | core `trigger_level_exact` (= `verdict.rejection_level`) |
| triggers fired (C6) | ledger | core `triggers`; extra setups from `extra_signals[i].triggers`; fleet via `build_shared_signal`'s own block mapping |
| trigger bar close (C4) | ledger | core `spy` — which **is** `bc["bar"]["close"]` in `_build_payload` |
| envelope (C4) | bars | the same 150-bar RTH window the live shadow uses |
| `level_states` bounce history (C3) | bars | `heartbeat_core._rebuild_level_states`, the engine's own stateless port |
| confluence zones (C7) | partly regenerated | `confluence_producer.build_zones` over 3 of its 4 sources |

**The clock wrinkle the task flagged, solved by avoiding it.** `refresh_levels_intraday.refresh(df=…)`
works but (a) *writes* `automation/state/key-levels.json` + `today-bias.json` on every call and
(b) stamps `now = et_now()`, labelling replayed levels with today's date and changing
`_level_expired`'s answer downstream. Both were sidestepped rather than patched: this study uses
`reconstruct_levels_asof`, which calls the **same helper functions** with an explicit `as_of_et`
and performs **no I/O at all**. Expiry is then evaluated against the as-of date, mirroring
`_read_level_records`. `et_now()` never enters the path.

**No live state file was written — verified, not asserted.** `key-levels.json` and
`today-bias.json` *did* change during the run (mtime 20:33:37 local), so the claim was checked
rather than trusted: the file's own `as_of` is `2026-08-12T22:33:37-04:00` with
`computed_from: refresh_levels_intraday.py (live Alpaca REST…)` and spot 772.30, and
`Gamma_LevelRefresh` (trigger `PT5M`, duration `P3650D` — it fires after hours too) has
`LastRunTime = 20:33:36`, one second earlier. That is the scheduled producer doing its normal
job, coincident with this run. The proof from the other side: the only write targets in all
four tools are `analysis/conviction/*` and one file in the system temp dir; the sole occurrence
of `refresh(` in this study is inside a docstring explaining why it is not called; and
`rli.MEMORY_MAP` is repointed in-process only and restored in a `finally`.

### Reconstruction fidelity — measured, not claimed

Replayed at all 28 real `key-levels-history` snapshot instants (14 dates × ~2 usable RTH times):

- **mean recall 0.852** — share of the real file's in-scope active levels the reconstruction found
- **mean precision 0.806**

Per-entry, against the engine's own logged `levels_active` (73 checkable rows):
**79.5% agreement, 15 rows where the reconstruction missed a level the engine really had,
and 0 rows where it invented one.** The reconstruction is strictly conservative: it can only
*understate* C1. Forcing those 15 rows to C1 = +2 (H4) does not change any conclusion —
signal-level Spearman moves to -0.12, the score-5 bucket is untouched, the ratchet result is
byte-identical.

| arm | scorable | dropped | scorable P&L | lanes |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| safe-2 | 63 | 5 | -$617 | core_main 28, core_extra 35 |
| risky-1 | 49 | 22 | +$255 | fleet, fleet_extra |
| risky-3 | 48 | 31 | +$180 | fleet, fleet_extra |

| bold-2 | 19 | 4 | -$776 | core_main |
| safe-1 | **0** | 27 | — | fleet (all pre-2026-07-10) |

**All 109 drops have one cause:** `trigger_level_exact` was added to the core row by G12 and is
absent from every row before **2026-07-10** (verified by per-date key census: 0 rows carry the
key before 2026-07-09, 794/794 on 07-10). For a level-anchored entry from that era the level it
fired against was never written down. Re-deriving it by re-running the detector over a
*reconstructed* level set would be a second-order guess dressed as history — **refused**.
Dropped cohort: -$1,278 realized.

### Three per-arm data shapes, and why the prior attempt's zeros were fake

The earlier run scored 10 of 37 entries at 0 because `placement_configs()` reads `trigger_level`
off the **fleet placement block — a key fleet rows do not carry**. Fleet arms do not need it:
`build_shared_signal` feeds them the core row's `triggers_fired` / `trigger_level_exact`
verbatim. Each arm is therefore resolved through the lane that actually holds its data:

1. **core_main** (47) — full fidelity.
2. **core_extra** (35) — the **second execution path** the design memo listed as unresolved.
   `extra_exec[i].exec.status == "PLACED"`, 36 placements from the extra-setup watchers. On
   these rows the top-level `triggers` is `[]` and `trigger_level_exact` is `null` *because
   they describe the core setup, which did not fire*. Reading them is precisely the fake-zero
   trap; this lane reads `extra_signals[i]` instead. These setups have **no level anchor at
   all** — a TRUE C1 zero, flagged `level_anchored_setup: false`.
3. **fleet / fleet_extra** (87 / 36) — joined back to the core tick via
   `build_shared_signal._bold_passed_blocks_from_row`, the producer's own function.
   Joining on `verdict.startswith("ENTER")` fails on 106 of 223 fleet fills, because
   `_score_peak_check` passes a side on score+trigger even on a HOLD tick (SCORING_PEAK_LIVE).
   `fleet_extra` = `VWAP_CONTINUATION`, emitted by an inline live `fleet_market` detector pass
   whose output was **never persisted**: triggers there are `None` (unrecoverable), never `[]`.

### Nothing scores 0 from a missing input

Every row carries `upper_bound` = total + the weight of every component that could not be
measured, and a block is called **robust** only when `upper_bound < floor` — i.e. no missing
input could have flipped it. Of 184 blocks, **158 are robust**; 26 could in principle flip.

Two components are unmeasurable and are named on every affected row (201 of 205):
`elite_trigger` (fleet_extra lane only) and `zone_stack` (rebuilt from 3 of 4 sources — the
`trendlines-live.json` source was never snapshotted, so C7 is a strict **lower bound**).
`structure_agreement` is 0 on 100% of rows, which is **live-faithful, not a gap**: production's
own shadow passes `structure_side=None`. **The reachable maximum today is 7, not 8** — in this
study and in production.

### Artifact hunts — the finding survives all of them

| hunt | result |
|---|---|
| **H1 setup-class confound** — 71 of 99 zeros are extra-setup fills that structurally cannot score | **Ruled out.** Extra-setup cohort *loses* (-$1,834, WR 0.239). Level-anchored alone is *positive* (+$602, WR 0.313) and the correlation is unchanged (-0.169 per fill, -0.025 per signal). |
| **H2 day concentration** | **Ruled out.** The score-≥5 cohort loses on **all 5** days it appears (-22 / -80 / -628 / -310 / -259). Drop-worst-day still -0.101 mean RoR; drop-best-day -0.158. |
| **H3 arm confound** | **Ruled out.** Negative in all five arms independently: bold-2 -0.44, safe-3 -0.22, safe-2 -0.20, risky-1 -0.15, risky-3 -0.14. |
| **H4 C1 under-count** | **Does not rescue it.** +2 forced on the 15 known-missed rows: Spearman -0.12, score-5 bucket unchanged, ratchet identical. |
| **H7 independence** | **This is the real limit.** 205 fills = 88 signals. The score-≥5 cohort is **6 signals, 0 winners**, 24 mirrored fills. At the level-anchored baseline signal WR of 0.294, P(0 of 6) = **0.124** — suggestive of harm, **not significant**. |
| **H8 role-side coherence** (conviction.py's own named calibration candidate) | **Not supported.** Coherent (call-at-support / put-at-resistance) is *worse*: 4 signals, WR 0.067, -$2,349, vs incoherent 18 signals, WR 0.263, +$163. ## 4. The ratchet counterfactual, with the matched control

Simulated **forward** over each (arm, day) entry stream in clock order — k starts at 0 and
increments **only on entries the rule takes**, mirroring `entries_used_today`. Historical fills
are never marked independently.

| | floor 5 ratchet | population |
|---|--:|--:|
| entries kept | 21 of 205 (6 of 88 signals) | 205 |
| kept P&L | -$1,210 | -$1,232 |
| **kept expectancy/trade** | **-$57.62** | **-$6.01** |
| **kept win rate** | **0.095** | **0.288** |
| kept mean return-on-premium | -0.130 | +0.023 |
| suppressed P&L | **-$22** | — |

### 🚨 Matched suppress-k-at-random control (20,000 draws, per (arm, day) cell)

| | value |
|---|--:|
| **observed** suppressed P&L | **-$22** |
| null p05 | -$487 |
| null median | **+$159** |
| null p95 | +$835 |
| **p (observed ≤ null)** | **0.325** |

Removing 184 trades at random from the same cells removes a *median +$159* of P&L. The ratchet
removed -$22 — squarely mid-distribution. **The suppression carries no information.** Matching
on the cell (not just the day) also matches arm sizing, so a rule that happened to block the
biggest-size arm could not masquerade as selection skill.

## 5. F3 (C27 frequency gate)

> "floor-5 blocking >95% or <20% of ENTERs = dead on arrival"

- flat floor 5: **88.3% blocked → PASS**

## 6. What this changes

1. **The "NOT BACKTESTABLE" verdict was wrong and is corrected.** Level records regenerate
   as-of at 0.85 recall / 0.81 precision against real snapshots. Any future level-dependent
   study can reuse `backtest/tools/conviction_levels_asof.py`.
2. **Do not arm the ratchet on the strength of the 08-12 exhibit.** On 23 days of real fills the
   score has no measurable discrimination and the ratchet's kept cohort is worse than doing
   nothing at every floor tested.
3. **The shadow-forward plan stands, but F1's bar just got harder and more honest.** The
   sample unit must be the **signal**, not the fill — the 6-arm fan-out means "≥25 would-blocked
   fills" can be as few as 3 independent signals. F1 should read ≥25 blocked *signals*.
4. **Freeze weights on nothing yet.** Two of eight points are unreachable in production today
   (C5 never threaded; C7's zone file is produced and consumed by nothing), and `range_extreme`
   — the component the memo calls the discriminator for J's 12:35 bounce — fired on **7.3%** of
   entries and lost money when it did (4 fills, -$572). The weights are not calibrated; they are
   guesses that this population declines to support.
5. **One concrete engine bug found in the scorer.** `conviction.py`'s C2 tests
   `str(rec.get("source","")).startswith("shelf")`, but the producer writes
   `source = "daily_context_shelf"` — the prefix never matches. Multi-week shelf zones (weight 5,
   the highest-conviction structural level the engine has) therefore score **0** on the
   multi-day-memory component unless they separately carry `memory_score`/`multi_day`/a
   `MEMORY_` label, which they do not. C2 currently fires only for `level_memory` records.

## Disclosures that travel with every number

1. **109 of 314 positions (35%) are out of scope** — `trigger_level_exact` predates 2026-07-10.
   The scorable window is 2026-07-10 → 2026-08-12 (2026-07-02/06 contribute extra-setup rows only).
2. **C7 is a lower bound** (trendline source unrecoverable); **C5 is structurally 0** (live-faithful).
   Reachable max is 7.
3. **C1 is understated on ~20% of checkable rows** and never overstated. H4 quantifies the impact.
4. **Fills are not independent.** 205 fills = 88 signals. Every per-fill statistic is quoted
   alongside its per-signal counterpart; where they disagree, the per-signal number is the real one.
5. **Premarket-curated levels are only present on the 14 snapshot dates**; elsewhere the
   reconstruction is a conservative subset, which can only lower C1/C2.
6. **Trigger-bar provenance:** 162 of 205 rows use the row's own logged `trigger_bar_et`;
   18 are recovered exactly by matching the logged `spy` to a 5m bar close; **25 fall back to
   the engine's `trig_idx = n-2` geometry** and are tagged `as_of_source:
   derived_trig_idx_n_minus_2` in the JSONL. A wrong bar there shifts the reconstructed level
   set by one 5m bar, not by a session.
7. Realized broker P&L is the oracle throughout. No simulated exits, no modelled fills.
