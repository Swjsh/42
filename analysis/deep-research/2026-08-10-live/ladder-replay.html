What changed in the engine

The rungs ship as specified

+50% → floor +30%. +75% → floor +60%. Armed on high-water mark alone, never referencing TP1, and the floor never lowers. This is what breaks the old chain, where protection was gated behind a +100% take-profit that 0DTE almost never reaches.

The trail moved from +40% to +75%

At a +40% arm a 20% trail can only out-floor the +50 rung while gain sits between +40% and +50% — and in that band it liquidated every 773C at ~10:05 on the first pullback. Day came to +$132 instead of +$446. Armed at the top rung it only takes over above +100%, covering the gap where the fixed floor would otherwise let a +200% runner give back to +60%.

Five-minute granularity. Floors are tested against each bar's low. Where one bar both sets a new high-water mark and breaches the floor that results, this resolves optimistically — arm first, then exit at the floor. Sub-bar ordering is unknowable from these bars.

No SPY feed in the replay. Structure and ribbon exits cannot fire, so the no-ladder control replays to −$1,813 against a real −$758. Every number here is quoted against the actual fills, never against that control.

This is one day, n=1. A rung sweep on this tape happens to favour +60/+35 and +80/+65 by a further $154 — that difference is noise at this sample size and was not shipped. The population run against the frozen pre-registration is the gate that decides.
