## 1. THE REALISTIC GOAL (replaces the old 10-20%/day framing)

**$100-200/day at the ~$2K tier = success.** The arithmetic that makes it concrete:
- 30% risk cap at $2K ≈ $600 position ≈ 3 contracts at ~$1.00-1.50 premium.
- ONE clean +30% premium move = $135-180. **One good level trade a day pays the goal.**
- Percent != contract price != dollars: a +30% premium target off a good entry is quick and
  repeatable; chasing dollar amounts forces oversizing/overtrading (C31: the killer was always
  sizing-up/adding, never flat count). Futures (no theta) are a different arithmetic — keep the
  lanes' math separate, never transplant option targets onto futures or vice versa.
- Scaling comes from COMPOUNDING the tier ($2K -> $5K -> $10K), never from forcing more trades
  per day at the same tier. Anti-goal: the 2026-07-21/22 open-churn shape (3 entries/25 min,
  -$76/-$108) is the exact opposite of one-good-trade.

## 2. THE RESEARCH SCOPE: LEVELS FIRST (bounded search)

J's standing conviction, now with our own supporting evidence (winning-trade map 07-13..20:
entries <$0.25 from a known level: +$340; entries $0.25-1 away: -$254):
- **Primary research lane = level interaction**: rejection, reclaim, S/R flip + retest,
  range ping-pong between adjacent levels, break-and-retest. Zones, never penny-exact
  (feedback_levels_are_zones).
- Chef/kitchen/prospector intake: level-family candidates get priority; a non-level candidate
  needs a stated reason it can't be expressed as a level interaction, or it queues BEHIND all
  open level-family work. (Enforcement: CHEF-FOCUS-FILTER queue item.)
- **Over-engineering tells (any of these = stop and simplify):** a candidate with >4 tunable
  parameters; a gate stacked on a gate to rescue a weak base signal; a strategy that cannot be
  stated in two sentences of chart language; a grid whose winning cell can't explain WHY in
  market terms; adding a new indicator when a level + candle already expresses the idea.
- The infinite-possibility space is the enemy: the standing kill-discipline (pre-reg, immutable
  ship bars, honest nulls) stays exactly as is — this doctrine narrows WHAT enters the pipe,
  not the bar for leaving it.

## 3. WHAT "GOOD" LOOKS LIKE FOR RANKING (realism lens on batteries)

When ranking candidates that clear gates, prefer, in order:
1. **Day-consistency** (majority-of-days winners, low concentration) over total P&L.
2. **Per-trade expectancy at 1-trade/day cadence** over high-frequency edge.
3. **+30%-class capture off level entries** over runner-fantasy targets (C30: unconstrained
   runner targets are dead knobs).
This is already mostly encoded in the standing 4-condition ship bar — cite this section when
adjudicating ties instead of inventing new metrics.

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE

Kill switches, risk caps, the 10 rules, pre-reg discipline, paper-first: untouched. This is a
focus lens, not a rule change. Rule 9 applies to any params edit that follows from it.
