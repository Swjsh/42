# Lesson candidate: two bar-walk conventions for the SAME trade — fill bar included (t4 replay family) vs excluded (simulator_real) — and the one combination that fabricates wins

**Symptom:** Building `p5_topcell_real_fills_confirm.py` (2026-07-11), a P5 top cell (OTM-1, −8% stop, trailing-15% lock, zero arm threshold) replayed at −$20.23/tr through `t4_exit_matrix._load_bars` but +$25.62/tr with the fill bar excluded — a SIGN FLIP on identical trades. The same loader had already fed T4/T5 and the STOP-A/STOP-B decisions (kills of exit-A/exit-B/entry-1+exitA; ships of the $0.30 floor + vwap port).

**Root cause:** `t4._load_bars` masks `ts >= entry_ts` — bars[0] IS the fill bar and `replay()` checks its own H/L — while `simulator_real.simulate_trade_real` walks exits from `entry_idx_opt + 1` (one-full-bar min hold). Neither is wrong: t4's is closer to the live 1-min exit actuator (which can act inside the first 5 minutes); the sim's is the convention every P5 cell/grind/ship-gate was graded under. The failure mode is MIXING them silently (T4's P5-survivor gate column literally compares across conventions) and one structurally unsafe case: a LIMIT fill mid-bar replayed against that bar's FULL H/L credits prints that may predate the fill (same-bar TP1 look-behind).

**Audit result (2026-07-11, `analysis/recommendations/entry-exit-matrix-fillbar-audit-2026-07-11.md`):** reproduced T5 layer (a) to the penny, re-ran fill-bar-excluded: ZERO verdict flips; the 3 kills byte-identical (0 trades changed — stops re-touched later at the same fill level); floor ship REINFORCED (lift +$13.71→+$18.87); vwap port never exposed (its gate used simulate_trade_real). ONE narrative kill: exit-C+entry-2's "beat control on the fresh slice" was two same-bar TP1 credits after limit fills (bar-0 high +50.6% vs fill, post-fill-bar MFE −5.5%); excluded, it UNDERPERFORMS control (−$14.73→−$138.26). Tight-stop absolute numbers are the sensitive class: control's burned-pop expectancy carries ~$12.63/tr of bar-0 noise-touch penalty (45% of fills touch −20% inside the first 5 minutes — the T2 noise-floor mechanism, quantified).

**Fix (2026-07-11):** semantics deliberately UNCHANGED (published T3/T4/T5 artifacts stay reproducible; live-parity argument stands). Divergence made loud instead: FILL-BAR CONVENTION disclosure block in t4's docstring + disclosures output + `_load_bars` docstring; KNOWN-HAZARD docstring on `t5.replay_entry2_pair`; guard `backtest/tests/test_fill_bar_convention.py` (4 tests) pins BOTH conventions + both disclosure blocks so either side changing REDs into a conscious re-audit.

**Generalization:** the profit-lock-scope lesson's sibling (2026-07-09): when two engines replay "the same trade," parity needs an explicit check per AXIS — knob scope, fill price, AND the management-window boundary (which bar may first exit). Grade a cell under one convention, confirm it under the same one; any cross-convention comparison (gates, anchors, parity tables) must name the divergence or be treated as unmeasured. Sharpest sub-rule: limit/stop-triggered fills must never be managed against their own fill bar's extremes on bars coarser than the fill process — that's look-behind, and it manufactures TP1s.
