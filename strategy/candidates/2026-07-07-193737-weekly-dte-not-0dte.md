# Strategy candidate: Weekly hold (1-2 DTE) instead of 0DTE — the C3 nail is a same-day-expiry artifact

> DRAFT — Chef proposal 2026-07-07 19:37 ET. J ratifies.

## Hypothesis
J's directional edge is real (beats random every test) but died in ~6 prior options
backtests on the C3 theta/premium nail. Every one of those bought **same-day-expiry
(0DTE)** contracts. Claim: the C3 nail is a **0DTE artifact**. Buy the same signal's
contract at **longer DTE (1-2 days, weekly)** — same strike, same exit logic — and the
lower per-day theta lets the move pay before decay eats it. The ONLY variable changed
is DTE/hold; entry trigger + strike offset + exit rules are held constant.

## Disclosures (per OP-20)
1. **Account-size assumption:** ITM-2 = the $2-10k production strike tier (matches Safe-2
   / Risky-2 sizing). QTY=3, real premiums $1-6.
2. **Sample-bias disclosure:** 166 signal-days over 18 months; OOS = 2026 (50-51 obs/bucket).
   Signal is the LIVE vwap_continuation watcher, not a mined-for-this-test trigger.
3. **Out-of-sample:** OOS exp monotone up in DTE ($36→$59→$66); the whole result IS the
   OOS split. Cross-family (vwap_reclaim) OOS flips positive only at 2DTE.
4. **Real-fills check:** real OPRA day-T bars for entry/management; expiry settled at pure
   intrinsic on real SPY close (no synthetic mid-life marks). Overnight gap modeled at T+1
   open (gap-through-stop honored). held-overnight ~0% so gap risk barely bites at -8%.
5. **Failure-mode enumeration:**
   - The cache is **entry-day-only per DTE tier** — DTE=2 is the LONGEST honest bucket
     without a new fetch. A true 3-4 DTE Friday weekly (J's actual 745P was 3 DTE) needs
     an `options_3dte`/`options_4dte` backfill (~8,090 Alpaca calls, ~40 min @ 200/min).
   - The -8% premium stop means the move must work FAST intraday-day-T; a slower multi-day
     grind that a real weekly-holder would ride is NOT captured (held% ~0). A wider stop +
     multi-day hold could be even better OR gap-exposed — untested here.
   - OP-16 anchors are 0DTE PUTs; this study does NOT re-score edge_capture on the weekly
     — that requires the 3-4 DTE fetch for the anchor dates.
   - Paper key can't fetch <15-day-old options data (403) — live weekly validation blocked
     until data ages or a SIP-entitled key is used.
6. **Concentration:** top5_pct 21-30% across buckets; drop-top3 stays near full mean.
